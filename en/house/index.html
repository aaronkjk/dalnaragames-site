About

A family has moved into Zelkova House, a hanok (traditional Korean house) that sat empty for 30 years. Every night, ghosts come in through the front gate, and you keep them out together with the gods who guard the house (gasin).

Ads only play when you tap a button for them.

How to play

  1. 1

    Place a house god on an empty spot in the yard

  2. 2

    It chases ghosts away on its own

  3. 3

    A ghost that reaches the main room puts out a candle. When they’re all out, you lose

Gods & ghosts

  1. 01
    Seven house gods

    From Jowang who guards the kitchen and Munsin who guards the gate to Samsin Halmeoni, they wake one by one

  2. 02
    Ten kinds of ghosts

    Plus three bosses

  3. 03
    Twenty nights of story

    With old customs like the Shoe Thief on Lunar New Year’s night and cracking nuts on the first full moon

  4. 04
    Grandma’s requests

    Three every night

  5. 05
    Tonight

    The yard and the rules change every day

  6. 06
    Endless Night

    Opens after the sixth night. The weekly ranking goes by how many waves you hold out
