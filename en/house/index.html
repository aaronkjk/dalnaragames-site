Ghosts, Begone!

Home Defense · A pixel hanok defense game

A family has moved into Zelkova House, a hanok (traditional Korean house) that sat empty for 30 years. Every night, ghosts come in through the front gate, and you keep them out together with the gods who guard the house (gasin).

Place a house god on an empty spot in the yard and it chases ghosts away on its own. Each ghost that reaches the main room puts out a candle, and when they’re all out, you lose.

  • There are seven house gods. They wake up one by one, from Jowang who guards the kitchen and Munsin who guards the gate to Samsin Halmeoni
  • Ten kinds of ghosts, plus three bosses
  • The story runs for twenty nights, with old customs like the Shoe Thief on Lunar New Year’s night and cracking nuts on the first full moon
  • Grandma has three requests every night
  • There’s a daily “Tonight” where the yard and the rules change every day
  • Clear the sixth night to open Endless Night. The weekly ranking goes by how many waves you hold out
  • Ads only play when you tap a button for them
  • For now the game is in Korean only

It's in closed testing on Google Play right now. Once it's officially out, I'll put the download link here.
