Privacy Policy

Effective December 3, 2026 · Posted November 26, 2026

Introduction

Dalnara Games (operated by JKCOMMERCE; “the Company”) handles the personal information of people who use its games, apps and website with care and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and other relevant laws. This policy applies to all games made by the Company (including Just One More Fish (한 마리만 더), Bouncy Skip (통통 물수제비), Stair Legend (계단의 전설) and Ghosts, Begone! (잡귀야 물러가라)), to its apps (including the workout log app BBAKFIT (빡핏)) and to the dalnaragames.com website.

1. Information we collect

The Company’s games and apps have no sign-up, and we do not separately collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name, phone number, address or email. However, the following information may be processed.

2. Purposes of use

  • Providing the games and saving game progress
  • Recommending workout routines and weights, saving workout records, and calculating daily calories and nutrients (BBAKFIT (빡핏), all on your device)
  • Providing the weekly ranking (showing the ranking board, calculating your rank, updating records from the same device)
  • Showing ads, giving rewards for watching ads, measuring ad performance
  • Confirming and restoring purchases of paid items
  • Responding to inquiries, fixing errors, preventing abuse

3. Retention period and destruction

  • Game progress: stored on your device and deleted immediately when you delete the app or clear the app’s data.
  • BBAKFIT (빡핏) workout records and body data: stored on your device and deleted immediately when you delete the app or clear the app’s data. A copy saved by Android Auto Backup can be deleted in your Google account’s backup settings. You can also delete them with Delete everything in the app and Delete body info in the body data section.
  • Weekly ranking records (anonymous ID, word-combination name, score, time recorded, app version): there is no automatic deletion, so they are kept while the weekly ranking is in operation and destroyed without delay if you request deletion. They are also destroyed when the weekly ranking service ends.
  • Anonymous sign-in accounts: deleted 30 days after they are created, by Google Firebase’s automatic clean-up feature. The anonymous ID and name stored on your device are deleted immediately when you delete the app or clear the app’s data.
  • Inquiry emails: kept for one year after we finish replying, then destroyed.
  • Information that must be kept under relevant laws (e.g. records of e-commerce transactions) is kept for the period set by law.
  • When information is destroyed, it is erased in a way that cannot be recovered.

4-1. Use of YouTube API Services

We use YouTube API Services to upload and manage videos of our own games on our official YouTube channel (Dalnara Games). This is an internal tool used only by us. It does not access the YouTube accounts of our players or website visitors, and it does not collect their information.

  • Features used: uploading videos to our channel, setting thumbnails, and checking our own channel information.
  • Data handled: we keep only information about videos we uploaded to our channel (video ID, title, upload date) in our internal records. We do not store or share anyone else's personal information.
  • Because we use YouTube API Services, the YouTube Terms of Service (opens in a new window) and the Google Privacy Policy (opens in a new window) also apply.
  • Revoking access: access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page (third-party apps with account access) (opens in a new window).
  • Deletion: to ask us to delete information we keep, email us at the address in section 9 and we will delete it without delay.

5. Turning off personalized ads

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, etc.; menu names may differ slightly by device). In regions where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area and the UK, we ask whether you consent to ads the first time you launch the game, and if you do not consent, we show only non-personalized ads.

6. Children’s personal information

The Company’s games are not mainly intended for children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect children’s personal information. If we find out that a child’s information has been collected, we delete it right away.

7. Your rights

You may at any time ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop its processing. Send your request to the email address below and we will handle it without delay. You can delete game information stored on your device yourself by deleting the app. When asking us to delete a weekly ranking record, tell us the game name, the name shown on the ranking board, the record and the week, and we will find that record and delete it.

8. Measures to keep information safe

We handle only the minimum information needed, and we manage inquiry emails only in an account with restricted access.

If this policy changes, we will post a notice on this page from 7 days before the change takes effect. Before adding anything new, such as an analytics tool, we will update this policy first.
