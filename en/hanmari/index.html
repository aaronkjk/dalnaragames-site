Just One More Fish

한 마리만 더 · A pixel fishing game

A game where you take a boat out to sea and catch fish. The ones you catch go into your aquarium so you can watch them.

The Korean name was picked by a vote on Threads. Exactly one comment came in, saying it’s a name that makes you want to keep fishing, so I went with it lol

  • Set the hook right when the bobber dips under
  • There are 5 seas and 76 kinds of fish. 12 of them only show up in their own month
  • Every catch adds a fish picture to your collection. Complete a whole sea and you get a trophy
  • Dress up the aquarium with decorations and backgrounds. Visitors come even while the game is closed
  • Daily check-in stamps and missions
  • Playable in Korean, Japanese, Traditional Chinese and English
  • No gacha. Ads only play when you tap a button for them

It's in closed testing on Google Play right now. Once it's officially out, I'll put the download link here.
