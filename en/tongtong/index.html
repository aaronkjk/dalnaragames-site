Bouncy Skip

통통 물수제비 · A bunny skipping stones

A chubby bunny skips stones on a moonlit lake. A round is over in a few seconds, so it’s good for a quick go now and then.

The stones bounce and the bunny looks pretty bouncy too, so I called it Bouncy Skip lol

  • Swipe up to throw. Tap right as the stone touches the water and it skips one more time
  • Pick whichever of the 4 throwing styles feels easiest
  • There are 8 places and each one is different, like a creek at sunset or a lake in the snow
  • 27 kinds of stones. Pick them up by the water or buy them with coins. One even looks like a Korean moon jar
  • You can put a scarf and a gat (a traditional Korean hat) on the bunny
  • Daily stamps and missions, plus 30 achievements
  • Playable in Korean, Japanese, Traditional Chinese and English
  • No gacha. Ads only play when you tap a button for them

It's in closed testing on Google Play right now. Once it's officially out, I'll put the download link here.
