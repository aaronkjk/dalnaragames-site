Contact

Game talk or bugs, send them by email.

If you get stuck or something looks wrong in a game, let me know by email. Tap the button below to open your mail app.

Write an email

dalnaragames.official@gmail.com

Good to include

  • The game’s name
  • Your phone model (e.g. Galaxy S24). You can find it in Settings under “About phone”
  • A screenshot. If something on the screen looks wrong, take a picture of it and attach it

A line or two about what happened is plenty.

Reply

I’ll reply as soon as I can. I make these games by myself, so it may take a little while.

To delete a weekly ranking record

Tell me the game, the name shown on the ranking, the score and the week. I’ll find it and delete it.

Your email address and message are only used to reply to you, and are deleted one year after I reply. See the Privacy Policy for details.
