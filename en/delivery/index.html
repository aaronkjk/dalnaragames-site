About

The elevator in the apartment building is under maintenance, so you deliver chicken by the stairs.

From outside it’s a 15-floor building, and you also find out why the stairs never end lol

Ads only play when you tap a button for them.

How to play

  1. 1

    Your rider climbs one step at a time on their own

  2. 2

    Tap where the stairs turn

  3. 3

    Turn the wrong way and you miss a step, and that’s the end

Neighbors & riders

  1. 01
    7 neighbors

    Each has a 5-part story. See one to the end and that neighbor joins your deliveries

  2. 02
    17 riders

    Including a cat and a Shiba

  3. 03
    The rider’s story

    Opens a chapter at a time as your earnings pile up. The last chapter is opening your own chicken shop

  4. 04
    Floor 100

    Reach it and you get the title “Stair Legend”

  5. 05
    Weekly ranking

    Starts over every Monday
