Stair Legend

Chicken to the Rooftop · A pixel stair-delivery game

The elevator in the apartment building is under maintenance, so you deliver chicken by the stairs. Your rider climbs one step at a time on their own, and you just tap where the stairs turn.

Turn the wrong way and you miss a step, and that’s the end. From outside it’s a 15-floor building, and you also find out why the stairs never end lol

  • Pass a floor with a door and the delivery happens on its own. You get the delivery fee and tip envelopes
  • 7 neighbors each have a 5-part story. See one to the end and that neighbor joins your deliveries
  • 17 riders deliver with you, including a cat and a Shiba
  • As your earnings pile up, the rider’s story opens one chapter at a time. The last chapter is opening your own chicken shop
  • Reach the 100th floor and you get the title “Stair Legend”
  • There’s a weekly ranking that starts over every Monday
  • Ads only play when you tap a button for them
  • For now the game is in Korean only

It's in closed testing on Google Play right now. Once it's officially out, I'll put the download link here.
